Course · PHYS-1410 / PHYS-2110

Physics I

A detailed study of one- and two-dimensional motion: kinematics, Newton's Laws, energy, momentum, and rotational motion, with oscillations, fluids, and thermal physics as time allows. Taught in two tracks — PHYS-1410 (Algebra & Trigonometry) and PHYS-2110 (Calculus) — meeting together for lecture and separately for recitation.

Unit 3 — Momentum & Rotational Motion Weeks 9–12 · Exam: Week 12

How motion is conserved and how it transfers in collisions, a review of uniform circular motion, and the rotational counterparts of displacement, velocity, force, and momentum.

TrackReading
PHYS-1410 (Trig) Chapters 8, 6 (§6.1–6.3 only) & 10 (in that order), College Physics 2e
PHYS-2110 (Calc) Chapter 9, a review of circular motion from Chapter 4, & Chapters 10–11 (in that order), University Physics Vol. 1

Uniform Circular Motion (College Physics 2e §6.1–6.3) is a review here — gravitation and satellites (§6.4–6.6) aren't covered in this course. Center of Mass and the rotational-collision/gyroscope material at the end of Chapter 10 are folded into the sections below rather than given their own subsection.

Unit 4 — Fluids & Oscillations Weeks 13–17 · Exam: Week 16

How fluids behave at rest and in motion, the physics of repeating (oscillatory) motion from springs to pendulums, and how that motion propagates outward as waves and sound.

TrackReading
PHYS-1410 (Trig) Chapters 11, 12, 16 & 17 (in that order), College Physics 2e
PHYS-2110 (Calc) Chapters 14, 15, 16 & 17 (in that order), University Physics Vol. 1

Not every section of these chapters is covered: Chapter 11 skips §11.1 and §11.8–11.9 (cohesion/adhesion and pressures in the body), and Chapter 12 stops after §12.3 — viscosity and laminar flow (§12.4+) is not covered in this course.
